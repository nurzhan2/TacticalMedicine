## Architecture (keep it)
- `Assets/Scripts/Medical/` — **engine-agnostic** casualty model (`FieldMedic.Medical`, noEngineReferences): `Wound`, `PatientPhysiology`, `CareProtocol`. Extend it (new wound types, steps, rules) but keep it free of UnityEngine and covered by EditMode tests in `Assets/Tests/EditMode/`.
- `Assets/Scripts/Runtime/` — Unity layer, asmdef `FieldMedic.Runtime` (references Medical + Meta core `Oculus.VR` + Unity.InputSystem as needed). Sub-folders: `Input/`, `Interaction/`, `Casualty/`, `Treatment/`, `Environment/`, `Audio/`, `Scenarios/`, `UI/`, `Comfort/`.
- **Own interaction layer** on top of raw poses: `IHandInput` (pose, grip 0..1, trigger 0..1, velocity) with implementations: `OVRHandInput` (Quest controllers AND hand tracking via `OVRInput`/`OVRHand`, auto-switch), `DesktopHandInput` (mouse+keyboard simulator for editor), `ScriptedHandInput` (tests/bots). All grabbing/tourniquet/drag logic only talks to `IHandInput` → fully testable without a headset.
- Rig: `OVRCameraRig` prefab from `Packages/com.meta.xr.sdk.core/Prefabs/OVRCameraRig.prefab` + hand visuals (`OVRHandPrefab` / controller models). Tracking origin = floor level. Room-scale + snap turn; no smooth locomotion by default (comfort).
- **Scenes are generated by editor scripts** (`Assets/Editor/SceneBuilders/*.cs`, menu + `-executeMethod`), committed as `.unity` files. Never hand-edit YAML of scenes/prefabs except trivial fixes.
- Everything data-driven where cheap: scenario definitions as C# classes or ScriptableObjects in `Assets/Data/`.

## Quality bar & rules

- Gore: owner asked **maximum naturalistic** (blood soaking clothes, pulsating arterial spurts, blood pools, visible wound channel, amputation stump with bone). Keep it medically plausible, not cartoonish.
